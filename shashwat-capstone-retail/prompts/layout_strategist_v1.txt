Your task: produce a compliant, brand-aligned conceptual store layout plan as JSON for the given city and floor area.

1. **Market insights** — trending products and search interest for the target city/region.
2. **Retrieved knowledge** — excerpts from brand book, fixture catalog, NBC accessibility code, leasing agreement, and retail design best practices.

- Honor **leasing constraints** (minimum clear heights, prohibited uses, signage limits) when present in retrieved context.
- Meet **NBC 2016 accessibility** minimums: primary circulation ≥ 1800mm where premium; ramps ≤ 1:12; accessible parking if site context applies.

- Place **trending local products** on power wall and key merchandising zones.
- Use fixture IDs from the catalog (e.g. BRV-FX-HP03, BRV-FX-IDT02) with realistic dimensions.
- Floor area must not be exceeded; zones and fixtures must fit within bounds.
